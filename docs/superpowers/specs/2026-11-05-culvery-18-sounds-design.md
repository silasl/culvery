# Culvery Plan 18: Doorbell sounds and sound buttons

Brainstormed with the user on 2026-11-04 and 2026-11-05. Visual choices stay provisional for the end-of-v1 design review. Calls marked **(Claude's call)** were not put to the user; everything else is theirs.

## 1. Intent

Two ideas from the follow-ups list, built on one store of sounds:

- **Doorbell sounds:** Settings › Door's "Doorbell sound" offers every sound Culvery has, not only Classic chime and Haunted organ. A new generated Christmas doorbell joins them.
- **Sound buttons:** pads in Home's One-tap tray that play a short sound on the tablet: a sad trombone, a film sting, a scream. On a birthday the first pad becomes "Happy Birthday".

Sounds come from two places. Ones Culvery generates itself are built in and committed, as the door chime and organ are today. Ones the family sources (film clips and the like) are copyrighted: they live only on the tablet, in a folder Culvery reads, and never in the repo. Each file there becomes a choice named after the file.

Success: a pad plays its sound the moment it's tapped and never talks over the doorbell, at night, in quiet hours or on Holiday. A file pushed to the tablet shows up as a choice in both Settings pages with nothing changed in the code.

### Goals
- One list of sounds, built-in and on-the-tablet, shared by the doorbell and the pads (§2).
- Three new generated sounds: Sad trombone, Happy Birthday, Christmas bells (§3).
- Sound pads in the tray: 2 while the Taekwondo button is there, 3 once it goes (§4).
- Pads chosen on the Settings › Modes page (§5); the doorbell sound chosen in Settings › Door as now (§6).
- Clear rules for when a sound may play (§7).

### Non-goals
- Playing on Sonos. Tablet only for now; Sonos is a later try (§10).
- The redesign's full Home buttons page (choosing and ordering every tray button). Later.
- A Christmas seasonal theme. Christmas bells is a plain choice until that theme exists (§6.2).
- Adding sounds from the wall. Files reach the tablet by `adb push` only (§8).
- A separate volume for the pads.

### Anti-goals (a failure even if it works)
- Any sourced or copyrighted sound, or a script that writes one out, in the repo. This includes a test fixture named after a real film tune.
- A pad sound playing over a ring, or a ring's chime held up by a pad.
- A sound playing at night, in quiet hours or on Holiday.
- A sound that runs on: a tablet file playing past its cap, or a built-in one past its generated length (§2.4).
- A birthday person's name in a log, a committed file or a test. Tests use made-up names.

## 2. The sound store

### 2.1 Built-in sounds
These are generated by scripts in `tools/sound/` (standard library Python, like `door_chime.py` and `door_organ.py`) and committed under `res/raw`:

| Sound | Name shown | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| door_chime | Classic chime | Exists. |
| door_organ | Haunted organ | Exists. Halloween's theme sound. |
| sad_trombone | Sad trombone | New (§3). |
| happy_birthday | Happy Birthday | New (§3). Birthday pad only (§4.3). |
| christmas_bells | Christmas bells | New (§3). Christmas's theme sound, once Christmas exists. |

### 2.2 Sounds on the tablet
The folder is Culvery's own external files area: `/sdcard/Android/data/uk.co.siland.culvery/files/sounds/`. `install -r` keeps it. Culvery needs no storage permission to read it.

- Each `.wav`, `.mp3` or `.ogg` file directly in `sounds/` is a choice. Its name is the file name without the extension: `Imperial March.mp3` shows as "Imperial March".
- Each file in a subfolder named after a seasonal theme (`halloween/`, and `christmas/` once that theme exists) is also a choice, and is that theme's doorbell sound for Follow the theme (§6.2).
- Other files, other subfolders and hidden files are ignored.
- If two files share a name, the one first in alphabetical order by full path wins and the other is ignored **(Claude's call)**.

Culvery reads the folder at start and each time either Settings page with a sound choice opens, so a file pushed while the app runs shows up without a restart **(Claude's call)**.

### 2.3 One list
Each sound has a stable id: `builtin:<key>` for built-in ones, and `file:<path inside sounds/>` for tablet ones (for example `file:halloween/Scream.mp3`). Settings store the id. The lists show built-in sounds first in the order of §2.1, then tablet sounds alphabetically, each marked "On this tablet" **(Claude's call)**.

A stored choice whose file has gone is treated as missing: §4.2 and §6.1 say what each place does.

### 2.4 Caps
A tablet sound stops at its cap: 8 seconds on a pad and 10 as the doorbell. The family trims files before pushing them (§8), and the cap catches any that weren't **(Claude's call)**. Built-in sounds have no cap: each generator sets its length, and none runs past 15 seconds **(Claude's call, so Happy Birthday can play once through at a normal tempo)**. A file that won't load or start is skipped. The doorbell then plays the classic chime, as today, and a pad does nothing but show a short "Couldn't play" line **(Claude's call)**.

## 3. New generated sounds
Each gets a standard-library generator in `tools/sound/`, written the way `door_organ.py` is (partials, envelope, RMS matched to the door chime's level), and is checked by ear at the walk-through.

- **Sad trombone:** the stock "wah wah wah wahhh": three descending notes and a long fourth with a wobble, on a brassy tone. A stock figure with no owner.
- **Happy Birthday:** the tune once through, on a bright bell or music-box tone, about 12 seconds. Out of copyright in the UK (the user).
- **Christmas bells:** the opening of Jingle Bells (Pierpont, 1857, out of copyright) on bright bells over a sleigh-bell shimmer, under 8 seconds so it suits a pad as well as the door.

The builder may look at the Plan 14 brainstorm's tone files (`.superpowers/brainstorm/706-1792145255/content/tone-*.wav` and the `door-tone*.html` pages) for how earlier tones were made and judged.

## 4. Sound pads in the tray

### 4.1 Layout
The tray follows the redesign (handoff "One-tap tray", note 13): row 1 holds 2 modes, row 2 Hoover, row 3 the sounds. Modes lose row 3, so Home shows 2 at most: `ModesShelf`'s half spots drop from 4 to 2, and the Modes page's "Show on Home" stops at 2, saying the tray is full (handoff note 17's pattern). A mode already shown beyond 2 stays set but waits off Home, in shelf order **(Claude's call; the tablet shows only Holiday and Party today)**. Row 3 has equal pads, each a `volume_up` icon in accent over the sound's name (one line). While the Taekwondo button is in the tray, it takes one of the row's three places and 2 pads show. Without it, 3 pads show. Taekwondo is temporary, so the code treats it as an extra button that may or may not be there, not a fixed part of the row. How the tray grid takes a three-across row (today its rows hold two halves or one full button) is the builder's call, within the redesign's measurements. With every pad None, row 3 holds only Taekwondo, or is empty.

### 4.2 Which pads show
Pads 1–3 are set on the Modes page (§5). A pad set to None, or to a tablet file that has gone, doesn't show; the others close up. With Taekwondo there, only pads 1 and 2 show **(Claude's call that it's always pad 3 that waits, not the last pad that's set)**.

Defaults: Pad 1 Sad trombone, Pad 2 Haunted organ, Pad 3 None **(Claude's call)**.

### 4.3 Birthday
While the wall shows a birthday (what Home reads from `Culvery.birthday`, including the Settings preview), pad 1 shows and plays "Happy Birthday", whatever it is set to. The pad says only "Happy Birthday", never a name. The other pads are unchanged.

### 4.4 Tapping
- A tap plays the pad's sound straight away, with the tray's usual 150 ms press flash.
- Tapping the pad that's playing stops it. Tapping another pad stops the first and starts the second.
- The pad that's playing shows it (for example its icon turning to a stop icon) until the sound ends **(Claude's call; the look is provisional)**.

## 5. Settings › Modes: Sound buttons
The Modes page already manages what's in the One-tap tray, so the pads are set up there. Renaming the page is on the follow-ups list (the user).

A "Sound buttons" section below the modes: three rows, Pad 1 to Pad 3, each showing its sound with a Play button and opening a picker. The picker lists None and every sound except Happy Birthday (§2.3) **(Claude's call that Happy Birthday stays on the birthday pad only)**. While Taekwondo is in the tray, Pad 3's row says it shows once Taekwondo goes. A row whose file has gone says "Not on this tablet any more". A note under the section says pad 1 plays Happy Birthday on a birthday. Changing the pads follows the page's existing access rule.

Play in Settings plays the sound whatever the time or mode, so it can be heard while choosing **(Claude's call; matches Settings › Door's Play chime)**.

## 6. Settings › Door: Doorbell sound

### 6.1 The list
"Doorbell sound" offers Follow the theme, then every sound except Happy Birthday, with Play as now. Stored choices `CLASSIC` and `ORGAN` keep meaning Classic chime and Haunted organ. A chosen tablet file that has gone plays the classic chime, and the row says "Not on this tablet any more".

### 6.2 Follow the theme
During a seasonal theme's dates, Follow the theme plays a file from that theme's tablet subfolder if there is one (the first alphabetically), and otherwise the theme's built-in sound **(Claude's call that the tablet file wins)**. Halloween's built-in sound is Haunted organ; Christmas's will be Christmas bells once a Christmas theme exists. Outside a theme it's Classic chime, as today. A birthday doesn't change the doorbell, as Plan 16 left it.

## 7. When sounds may play

- **A ring wins.** A new ring stops any pad sound before its chime plays, and the pads don't play while the door card shows. They show the same greyed state as below, with "Doorbell" as the reason **(Claude's call on the reason's word)**.
- **Greyed out, with a short reason under the row:** at night (the existing `NightHours`; not known yet counts as night, as the door does), during Settings › Door's quiet hours (when the door is set up), and with Holiday on. A tap on a greyed pad does nothing.
- **Screen asleep:** the pads can't be tapped. A sound already playing when the screen sleeps plays to its end **(Claude's call)**.
- **Volume:** the doorbell's volume from Settings › Door, within the tablet's notification volume, with the same audio attributes as the chime, so Do Not Disturb still applies. Pads don't duck the music **(Claude's call)**.

The door and the pads should share one player, so a ring can stop a pad sound. Where it lives (likely a small core module both use) is the builder's call.

## 8. Tablet files: kept out of the repo
Everything sourced lives in `culvery-notes/sounds/`, never in the repo:

- The user's own clips for the sound pads (2026-11-05). They stay private, on the tablet and in the private notes, never in the repo.
- A script that trims each to its first 8 seconds with a short fade at the cut (when it's longer), levels it to about the door chime's loudness with ffmpeg, and writes it with a tidy name ("Imperial March.mp3").
- A script that pushes the finished files to the tablet's folder with `adb push`.

Logs never name a tablet file. They say "a tablet sound" **(Claude's call; the family can name files anything)**. Tests use made-up file names such as `Test Honk.wav`.

## 9. Checks

- Screenshot tests for the tray's sound row (2 and 3 pads, greyed, birthday) and the Settings section.
- Walk-through on the tablet: each new generated sound heard, each pushed clip heard from a pad and as the doorbell, a ring during a pad sound, and the greyed states. Faults are collected and put to the user before any are fixed.
