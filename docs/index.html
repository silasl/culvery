Culvery

Culvery is an Android app for a tablet on the wall of a family home. It shows the household's shared calendar from Google Calendar and the weather, and lets the family add and change events from the tablet. It runs as a kiosk, so the tablet stays on Culvery, and each person has a name, a colour and a PIN that decides what they can do. It is for a household that builds and installs it on its own tablet; it is not on the Play Store.
