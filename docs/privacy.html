Culvery privacy policy

Last updated 5 October 2026

Culvery is an Android app for a tablet on the wall of a family home. This policy says what it stores, what it sends and to whom.

Who is responsible

Each household builds and installs Culvery on its own tablet and decides who uses it. That household is responsible for the information on the tablet. Culvery's developer runs no servers and receives nothing from the app: no data, no usage information, no messages.

What stays on the tablet

Culvery stores these on the tablet, in the app's own private storage:

  • the people the household adds: name, colour, role (Admin, adult or child) and PIN; the PIN is kept only as a salted hash, never as the digits;
  • the home location: the town's name, its coordinates and time zone;
  • calendar connections: the Google account's email address, the calendars in it with their names and settings, and a copy of the events from the day before today to about ten weeks ahead (the tablet shows the next four), with changes made on the tablet waiting to be sent;
  • the last weather forecast.

None of this leaves the tablet except as described below. Culvery turns off Android's app backup, so it is not copied to a cloud backup. Some tablets may still copy it to a new tablet when you move everything across with Android's own device transfer.

Google Calendar

When someone connects a Google account, Culvery uses Google's own sign-in on the tablet. Culvery asks for two permissions:

  • calendar.readonly, to read the account's calendars and their events, and show them on the tablet;
  • calendar.events, to add, change and delete events.

Culvery writes only to the one calendar the household chooses as its master calendar. When an event is for a person or added by a person, Culvery tags it with that person's internal id, a random code; it never writes people's names into Google Calendar. Sign-in tokens are handled by Google Play services on the tablet; Culvery does not store them. Calendar data goes only between the tablet and Google; nothing is sent to the developer.

Culvery's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Culvery uses that information only to show and edit the household's calendar on the tablet. It does not share it with anyone, use it for advertising, or sell it, and no person reads it except the household using the tablet.

Weather (Open-Meteo)

Culvery gets the weather from Open-Meteo, which needs no account or key. When the household searches for its town, Open-Meteo receives the text typed into the search. After that, each forecast request sends the home's coordinates and time zone. Beyond the tablet's IP address, which any web request reveals, nothing that identifies the household or its people is sent.

No analytics, ads or tracking

Culvery has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash reporting.

Logs

Culvery's log stays on the tablet. A release build writes only warnings and errors, and these contain no personal information: no names, PINs, email addresses, towns, coordinates or time zones.

Deleting your information

  • To delete everything Culvery stores, open Android's Settings › Apps › Culvery › Storage & cache and tap Clear storage (on some tablets: Storage › Clear data), or uninstall Culvery.
  • To remove Culvery's access to a Google account, go to myaccount.google.com/connections and remove Culvery.

Events Culvery added to Google Calendar stay there until you delete them in Google Calendar.

Children

Households using Culvery often include children. Culvery collects nothing from them beyond what the household enters for them: a name, a colour and a PIN, kept as a hash. These stay on the tablet.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
